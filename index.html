Apna Player

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026

In short: We do not collect your personal information, and we have no accounts or user database. Your videos and music stay on your device. The app shows ads provided by Google AdMob, and Google’s ad technology may collect certain device information to deliver those ads (see “Advertising” below).

Who we are

Apna Player (“the app”) is an Android video and music player developed by Talha Shahbaz (kinggtech) (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what the app does with information when you use it. If you have questions, contact us at talhashahbaz848@gmail.com.

Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information ourselves. The app has no sign-up or login, and it does not send your files, your viewing history or your settings to us or to any server we operate. We do not use analytics or crash-reporting services.

The only exception is advertising. The app includes the Google Mobile Ads SDK, which is operated by Google and may collect and process information as described in the “Advertising” section. We do not receive that information.

Advertising

Apna Player is supported by ads. The app shows full-screen (interstitial) ads served by Google AdMob. To load and show these ads, Google’s ad technology may collect and use information such as:

  • Your device’s advertising ID.
  • Your IP address and the approximate location that can be derived from it.
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version and app version.
  • Information about how ads are shown and interacted with, and diagnostic data.

This information is collected and handled by Google under its own policies, not by us. Please read Google’s Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

You can control ads on your device: in Android Settings you can reset or delete your advertising ID and opt out of ads personalization (the exact menu varies by device, usually under Settings → Privacy → Ads or Settings → Google → Ads). You can also manage Google ad settings at myadcenter.google.com.

Data stored on your device

To work properly, the app saves some information locally on your phone. The app does not upload this information:

  • Your settings, such as theme, default speed, skip time and auto-play options.
  • Playback positions, so videos can resume where you stopped, and the recent (continue-watching) list.
  • Your favorites and playlists.
  • Per-video preferences, such as aspect ratio, subtitle choice and timing, and A-B repeat points.
  • If you turn on the privacy features: a salted hash of your PIN (not the PIN itself) and the list of folders you chose to hide.
  • A cache of thumbnails, to make browsing faster.

This data is removed when you uninstall the app. You can also clear the recent history and the thumbnail cache from Settings.

Permissions the app uses

The app asks only for permissions that its features need:

PermissionWhy it is needed
Videos and audio (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO)To find and play the videos and music on your device (Android 13 and newer).
Storage (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)The same purpose on Android 12 and older.
Storage write (WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)To delete a file you choose to delete, on Android 9 and older.
Internet (INTERNET)To load and show ads from Google AdMob.
Advertising ID (AD_ID)Used by the Google ad SDK to serve ads (Android 13 and newer).
Foreground service and wake lockTo keep audio or video playing in the background and to stop the screen from sleeping during playback.
Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS)To show playback controls in the notification area.

The app reads your media files only to play them, show their names and thumbnails, and run the tools you choose. The files are not copied or sent anywhere.

Bluetooth and audio output

To keep sound and picture in sync, the app checks locally on your device whether audio is playing through a speaker, wired headphones or Bluetooth, and adjusts the audio delay automatically. This check happens on your device only, and the result is not sent anywhere.

Files the app creates

Only when you use these features, the app saves files on your device:

  • Screenshots are saved to Pictures/ApnaPlayer.
  • Videos created by the built-in tools (trim, compress, merge, split, mute and change music) are saved to Movies/ApnaPlayer, and extracted audio to Music/ApnaPlayer.

These files stay on your device, and you can delete them at any time.

Sharing

When you use the Share option, the app hands the file to Android’s share sheet, and you choose where it goes. We do not receive the file or know who you shared it with.

Third-party software

The app uses the Google Mobile Ads SDK to show ads, as described above. It also uses open-source media libraries, such as mpv and FFmpeg, to play and convert media; these run on your device. We do not include any other advertising or analytics software.

PIN lock and hidden folders

The PIN lock and hidden folders are convenience features that keep casual viewers out of the app. They do not encrypt your files, so they should not be relied on to protect highly sensitive content.

Children

The app is a general-purpose media player and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Your choices and deletion

Because we do not hold any of your data, there is nothing to request from us or ask us to delete. To remove everything the app has stored on your device, clear its data in Android settings or uninstall it. You can revoke any permission at any time in Android Settings → Apps → Apna Player → Permissions. For data handled by Google for ads, use the ad controls described in the “Advertising” section.

Changes to this policy

If the app’s data practices change, we will update this page and the effective date above. Please check it from time to time.

Contact

For any privacy question, write to Talha Shahbaz (kinggtech) at talhashahbaz848@gmail.com.
